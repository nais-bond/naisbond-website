The GRAND PRIX is back at WSC Den Bosch. And yes it's gonna be ONE HELL OF A CONTEST 😈

This year we're serving you HIT 25 TO BE ALIVE:
A menu of 25 tricks. Start easy, bail and you're out. 🍽️

Crews are already filming their GRAND PRIX: VAR edits in their favorite local parks this time. All premiere will be in the evening.

🕐 From 13:00 to 22:00
📍 World Skate Center, Den Bosch
🎯 Make Brabant skate again.

# How it works

Everyone starts with a Grand Prix wristband.
Miss a trick and your wristband gets cut.

We will hit two obstacles during the day. Which ones? You'll find out on March 21st.

If any riders make it through all 25 tricks, they move on to a Best Trick final to decide the winner.
