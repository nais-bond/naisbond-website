* 22-08 - Friday Night Skate - Big tour through the city lead by a car with a DJ on top!
* 23-08 - Free skate Clinics - We have skate rentals and teachers in the park for you.
* 24-08 - Vondelcup Main event - Showcasing the skate culture and getting new people involved.

for more information see https://www.thisissoul.com/pages/vondelcup

The VONDELCUP lets you enjoy the versatility of inline skating. Step out of your comfort zone, take on challenges in different disciplines, participate in fun competitions, and collect points to reach the finals. Do you have what it takes to take the VONDELCUP home?

Various disciplines of inline skating are coming together. Whether you are interested in regular Aggressive, Hockey, Speed, Slalom, or Flow skating, there is fun for everyone.

Come and watch some of the best skaters in the Netherlands in action. Each discipline has its own final competition to determine who the best skater is.

Join us on Friday for a special collaboration between the Amsterdam Friday Night Skate and Thisissoul Stoute Schoenen. We will make a tour through the city and visit some of the best skate parks where the Thisissoul team will give an aggressive inline skating demo.

USWA The VONDELCUP is part of the USWA event. The 6th edition of the Urban Sports Week (USWA) takes place. The USWA is the stage in the Netherlands where urban athletes can showcase their skills. Think of sports such as Inline Skating, 3x3 basketball, and freerunning. More information can be found at www.uswa.nl.

There are activities for skaters of all levels.

# SKATE LESSON FOCUSED ON THE FRIDAY NIGHT SKATE

## August 22 / Time: 19:00-20:00 / free / start: 19:00 / This is Soul, Overtoom 327

Do you want to feel safer during an FNS? Then come to our free skate lesson. We focus on adults who can already skate but want to improve their skills. No registration is needed. 

# FRIDAY NIGHT SKATE X URBAN SPORTS WEEK

## August 22 / Time: 20:15-23:00 / free / start: 20:15 / Vondel CS, Vondelpark

Skate through the city behind a car with a DJ on it! We will take you on a tour through the city. We will visit skate parks in Amsterdam and showcase some aggressive inline skating with a small demo and some tips for skating in the park if you dare to try. This activity is free and suitable for (adult) recreational skaters.

# FREE SKATE CLINIC IN VONDELPARK

## August 23 / Time: 11:00-17:00 / free / location: Vondelpark

We are organizing an open clinic in Vondelpark. This clinic is intended for all park visitors, from people who never skated to people who can already do a couple of braking teachnieques, from 5 to 60 years old. Come by and take part in our activities for free. No registration needed, just walk in!
